# Optional job level 50

The owner may allow job ranks 2–5 to progress to level 50. Novices remain capped at 10 and changing jobs still becomes available at the original level 40 requirement. Each level reached from 41 through 50 gives one skill point. The completed level of an earlier rank is stored separately when changing jobs, so advancing at 40 does not grant the ten optional points. Existing characters without that history retain the previous server assumption of level 40 for completed ranks.

The EXP needed at level 40 remains the game's configured value. For levels 41–49, the owner-selected extension adds the difference between the configured level 39 and 40 requirements for each subsequent level. This linear extension is a customization, not an original game rule. Hunting quests use 30% of the current level's job EXP requirement; they pay no job EXP at the applicable cap.

Characters who have passed level 40 keep their level and earned points if the option is disabled. The original cap applies to subsequent gains while disabled. The extended client must register its extra configuration rows before it can earn levels beyond 40.
